# Release acceptance matrix

Statuses: **VERIFIED** (implemented, tested, observed working end to end) · **PARTIAL** · **BLOCKED** (exact
blocker) · **NOT STARTED**. Updated at the end of every phase. Nothing unimplemented is marked VERIFIED.
Status here describes the target product in `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md`, not the milestone-1 scope, so several
rows that the MVP satisfied in a narrow sense are PARTIAL.

Last updated: 2026-11-13, Phase 12 S1–S4 and the history decision (row M revised; earlier rows as of their phase).
