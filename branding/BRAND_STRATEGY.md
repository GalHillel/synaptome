# Brand strategy, name research, voice

## Positioning

Synaptome is infrastructure, not an assistant: the layer that knows which capabilities exist, which are safe, and
which one a task needs. Its promise is **trust through evidence**. Every claim the product makes about a
capability can be traced to something recorded.

Audience: developers who work with AI coding agents daily and have more skills, plugins and MCP servers than they
can vet.

Not: a marketplace, a skill pack, a chat interface, or a rebrand of another project.

## Character

Exact, calm, unhurried. A well-kept ledger rather than a mission-control wall. It tells you what it does not know.

## Name

The product is called **Synaptome**: the complete map of the synapses in a nervous system. It names what the product
keeps, a map of every capability and the connections between them. Pronounced "sih-NAP-tome".

Clearance evidence, the alternatives that were considered and the open legal items are in
[`docs/NAMING_CLEARANCE.md`](../docs/NAMING_CLEARANCE.md). The name is not legally cleared; that is a release gate.

## Voice and copy

- Say what happened, with the number. "7 approved, 11 waiting, 1 blocked."
- Name the reason. "Waiting: licence unknown." not "Needs attention."
- Never round uncertainty up. "Not scored", "unknown", "blocked: OAuth session expired".
- Buttons are verbs for what will happen: Approve…, Install to sandbox, Roll back…. An ellipsis means a
  confirmation follows.
- Confirmations list consequences under "What this does".
- No exclamation marks, no "AI-powered", no "magic", no superlatives.
- Empty states explain what would put something there and how to do it.
- Errors give the cause and the next command.

Product terms, used consistently: capability, candidate, registry, quarantine, audit, tier, gate, approve,
install, sandbox host, real host, activate, gap, forge.
