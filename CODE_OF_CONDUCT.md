# Code of conduct

Synaptome is built in the open by people who disagree about technical things often and should be able to do so
without anyone being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expected

- Be specific and kind. Critique code, designs and claims, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding otherwise.
- Back claims with evidence: a test, a measurement, a reproduction.
- Make room for people who are new, who write in a second language, or who disagree with you.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language or imagery, in any channel connected to the project.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Using the project's channels to promote harmful capabilities (for example, skills built to exfiltrate data).

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or remove a participant from the project's spaces, in proportion
to what happened. Maintainers are held to the same standard.
